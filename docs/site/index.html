What makes it different

Six ideas, none of them a chatbot

01

Typed decisions with confidence bands

A decision is YAML: risk tier, enumerated options, an escape option, a default, a golden set. The model answers inside the options and reports calibrated confidence. The band decides whether it acts, asks an LLM to confirm, or opens a task. In the demo: 350 to 850 ms per call at about $0.00002.

02

Policy from a sentence

"Gold customers returning under $250 with a receipt get an instant refund" becomes a validated draft with a diff, generated golden cases and the approvals it needs. The copilot never publishes. The studio runs lint, impact diff, golden-set gate, separation of duties and a canary.

03

Margin replay of your own orders

Upload a Shopify CSV export and see what the policies would have saved: shipping avoided, margin recovered, splits avoided. A bundled sample runs without an upload. Everything stays on your machine.

04

Agent commerce built in

An MCP server with 11 tools, a UCP order capability, signed mandates with limits enforced before capture, injection screening that keeps instructions as data, and a one-click dispute evidence pack.

05

Explainability to customers

Every decision stores its full trace. Operators see the layer, the rules that fired, the band and the provider. Customers see a safe sentence derived from the same trace on the public tracking page.

06

Everything is data

State models, decisions, compliance rules, playbooks, SLAs, order types, nodes and rate cards are YAML per tenant, versioned, replayable from an append-only ledger, and editable at runtime behind a publish pipeline.

The decision fabric

Four layers. Autonomous exactly as far as the confidence allows.

Every decision passes through the same four layers. The risk tier (T0 to T3) sets the confidence bands; money-moving decisions act only above 0.9, recommendation-only decisions never act. Thresholds are changed by humans in the studio, never by a model or an agent.

L0

Deterministic rules

CEL expressions evaluated first, in priority order. The policy that must never be a judgement call: a flagged customer gets store credit, hazmat does not fly.

Match → act with confidence 1.0
L1

Typed decision model

Asked the question with enumerated options. Answers inside them and returns a calibrated confidence. Jev by default; any structured-output model behind the same interface.

Act band → apply · confirm band → L2 · else → L3
L2

LLM, strict JSON

Shown the trace and the same options. May only pick one of them. Anything else is discarded. Runs only in the confirm band on T1 and T2 decisions.

Valid option → apply · otherwise → L3
L3

A person

A task opens in a queue with the recommendation attached, an SLA and an escalation path. Every T3 decision ends here. Escalation is a first-class outcome, not an exception.

Decision recorded with actor and reason

Below the layers: kill switches per decision, per provider and for the whole AI layer; a per-tenant daily AI budget; a deterministic fallback provider and a default on every definition. With no key at all the system still captures, sources, ships and returns.

decision fulfilment_intent v1 tier T1 | layer L1_SYSTEM_ONE | band ACT | action act | value ship_complete | confidence 1.0 | provider jev decision return_risk v2 tier T2 | layer L3_HUMAN | band CONFIRM | action escalate | value 1.39 | confidence 0.66 | provider jev

Two lines from the captured demo run (docs/DEMO_SAMPLES.md): a gift note read as ship-complete, and a mixed-history return scored below the T2 act band and sent to a reviewer.

Who it is for

Retailers, OMS teams, agencies, researchers

  • Mid-market retailers replacing a spreadsheet-and-ERP order flow, or a first OMS whose rules nobody dares to touch. Start from a reference tenant and a Shopify export.
  • OMS teams building a new platform who want a decision layer, policy studio and ledger they do not have to design from scratch.
  • Agencies and integrators who want configuration to be versioned data they can test and hand over.
  • Researchers working on typed decisions, calibration and governance of LLM-assisted operations. Every record carries its trace, confidence and golden-set result.
